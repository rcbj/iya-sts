Natural Earth — public domain

Natural Earth data is in the public domain. The Terms of Use on
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/ state that all versions
of Natural Earth raster and vector map data found on that website are in the
public domain, that they may be used in any manner, including modifying the
content and design, electronic dissemination and offset printing, and that
no permission is needed to use them.

The data in admin-ui/natural_earth/ is derived from the 1:50m Admin 0 –
Countries dataset by tests/tools/natural-earth.js, which pins the upstream
file by SHA-256 and commit. Crediting "Made with Natural Earth" is not
required; this project does it anyway.
